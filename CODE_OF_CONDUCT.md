# Code of Conduct

Tubeline follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep feedback focused on the work. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background or experience. Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@jeffalldridge](https://github.com/jeffalldridge)). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, or contributors that violate this code.
