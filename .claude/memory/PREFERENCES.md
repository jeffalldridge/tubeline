# Tubeline Preferences

Workflow and style preferences learned across sessions. Newest first.

## October 22, 2026

- All writing uses American English and the *Chicago Manual of Style* and should read as if a person wrote it. Jeff gave this as a note to all agents; the rules are in the Writing section of AGENTS.md.
- Describe Tubeline as a tool for motion graphics apps in general. Apple Motion is the main example, not the only target.
- Jeff thinks in motion design terms (points, facets, strokes) and wants minimal settings with good defaults and visible feedback, like the triangle count and the preview.
- He prefers a flat solid color to shaded output.
- Edge cases matter (caps, dashes, fills, intersections), and they should be checked visually.
- His own test icons stay out of Git (`Samples/User`); our original samples are tracked.
- He wants performance-conscious assets that animate well in bulk.
